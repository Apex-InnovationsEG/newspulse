Privacy policy

NewsPulse is a desktop app for Windows. It keeps your data on your own PC. If you choose to sync, it puts an encrypted copy in your own Google Drive. We don't collect anything about you.

Last updated 28 October 2026 · Apex Innovations

What NewsPulse keeps on your PC

Everything lives in your Windows user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\NewsPulse), for your Windows user only:

  • Your account: a username and a hashed password (never the password itself), and your recovery code's hash.
  • Your setup: settings, topics, sources, saved posts and links with your notes, Saved lists, calendar, Event Pulse pages and your Markets list.
  • What NewsPulse collects for you: posts, videos and articles from the sources you add, and small copies of their pictures.
  • The credentials you enter (API keys, tokens, sign-ins for the live view), encrypted with a key kept in the same folder.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's email and name, and Google's sign-in token, encrypted and protected by Windows.
  • Logs, kept on your PC for fixing problems. They never contain your passwords, codes or tokens.

Nothing in this folder is sent to us. We have no servers that receive your data.

What goes to your Google Drive (only if you sync)

Sync is optional. You can use NewsPulse without Google.

  • Encrypted on your PC first. Your setup is encrypted (AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your sync password) before it leaves the PC. Google, and we, can't read it.
  • In your own Drive, in its hidden app folder. The copy goes to your Google Drive's hidden app data folder, which only NewsPulse can see. It doesn't appear in your Drive's file list and isn't shared with anyone.
  • What it holds: your setup (settings, topics, sources, saved items and notes, lists, calendar, Event Pulse pages, Markets list, look), your API keys and tokens, and your licence key. It keeps the last 30 versions so you can go back.
  • What it never holds: your NewsPulse password, your sync password, your Google sign-in, LinkedIn and Facebook sign-ins, the trial record, caches and pictures.
  • What NewsPulse asks Google for: your email address and basic profile (to show which account you signed in with) and access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive. Nothing else in your Drive: it can't see your files, photos or mail.

If you forget your sync password, the cloud copy can't be opened by anyone, including us.

NewsPulse's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What goes to news, price and update sources

To fill your feed, NewsPulse asks the sites, feeds and platforms you add for their latest posts, the same way a browser would. For the Markets list it asks price sources for prices, and it checks our release page for new versions.

These are plain requests for public information. NewsPulse doesn't send your name, email, account or any of your data with them. Like any request on the internet, the site you ask can see your PC's network address.

No tracking, no analytics

NewsPulse has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash reporting that phones home. We don't sell, share or look at your data, because we never receive it.

How to delete your data

  • On your PC: uninstall NewsPulse (Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps, NewsPulse) and answer Yes to "Also delete my data?". Or delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\NewsPulse after quitting NewsPulse from the tray.
  • The cloud copy: in NewsPulse, open Settings, Advanced, Account and choose "Delete my cloud data". You have 7 days to undo it; after that it is gone. Deleting the cloud copy never deletes what's on your PC unless you also choose that.
  • From Google's side: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings, Manage apps, find NewsPulse, then choose Options and "Delete hidden app data". You can also remove NewsPulse's access to your account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
