Your PC. Your data.

NewsPulse is a desktop app, not a website. It runs on your own computer and keeps everything there.

  • No cloud, no account with us. Your feed, topics and settings live in your Windows user folder.
  • Read-only. It never posts, likes, comments or sends messages for you.
  • No Google key needed. Add YouTube channels by name, or import your subscriptions from a Google Takeout file.
  • You're in control. Close the window and it keeps collecting in the tray. Choose Quit and everything stops.

Questions

Windows warns me when I run the setup. Is it safe?

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" for new apps that aren't signed with a certificate yet. Choose More info → Run anyway to continue. You can also compare the file's SHA-256 with the one above.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11. Mac and Linux aren't available yet.

Will it post or like anything for me?

No. NewsPulse only reads. It never posts, likes, comments or sends messages.

I forgot my password. What now?

On the sign-in screen choose "Forgot password? Use your recovery code" and set a new one.

How do I update to a new version?

NewsPulse checks for new versions itself and tells you when one is ready; it installs when you say so, never in the middle of a sync. You can also download the new setup and run it. Your feed, sources and settings stay as they are.

What happens when the trial ends?

NewsPulse switches to Free. Everything you collected stays; above the Free limits it is kept read-only until you remove some or get Pro.
